## Why the crowd column matters

[month-review](../month-review/) found that September's biggest price gains
and biggest crowd gains were mostly different coins: $RAY rose 134% with its
crowd up 1%. A price move with no audience behind it is a different event from
one with an audience, and only one of those is visible on a price screen.

This cohort has both, which is what separates it from most of this year's
bounces.

## The cohort is not a hand-picked list

Membership is LunarCrush's own `gaming` or `nft` tag, a peak market cap dated
2022 or earlier, and a drawdown of 90% or more. Every rule was written before
the returns were looked at. "The metaverse coins are pumping" is the easiest
narrative in crypto to assemble out of nine convenient names, so the cohort
here is 29 coins chosen by filter.

## Limits

One day. [graveyard](../graveyard/) made a similar cohort call on 2026-09-22
and the names fell a median 6.8% the next day.

Peak market cap comes from the cached history starting 2020-01-01, so a coin
whose true peak predates that has an understated drawdown. $ICP, which peaked
at $53B days after launching in May 2021, is inside the window and correct.

A 90% drawdown means small absolute numbers. $ILV's crowd going from 20 to 43
people a day is +115% and is still 43 people.
